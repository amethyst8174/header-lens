Why it's safe to drop real emails here
  • Processed on your device. The email is parsed in this tab. There is no server behind this page.
  • The browser blocks outbound traffic. A Content Security Policy forbids this page from sending requests, so neither the email nor its attachments can leave.
  • Nothing is opened or rendered. HTML is read as text, links are never followed, images and remote content never load, and attachments are only inspected as bytes.
  • Nothing persists. No cookies or browser storage. Leaving or reloading the page wipes everything.
